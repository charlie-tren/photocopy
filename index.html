Each morning a vision model is shown yesterday's photograph and nothing else - no earlier picture, no earlier words, no idea what it is looking at. It fills in the six slots above. Those six lines are the entire prompt for today's photograph. Then it is shown that one, and so on.

Loops like this are known to converge rather than wander: left alone they settle into a stock scene and stay there. Two rules push back, and both are generated from the chain's own past rather than supplied from outside - the fixed slots, which stop the description dissolving into atmosphere, and a ban on whatever words the chain has leaned on lately. Nothing else intervenes. There is one chain, it started once, and it is never reset.

Across the last 2 frames the descriptions are 38% alike and the pictures differ by 17 of a possible 64.
